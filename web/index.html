How to use
  • Pick a question set, or upload your own: several .txt / .docx files or a whole folder. Each file becomes its own set.
  • A file looks like this: 
    Câu 1: Thủ đô của Việt Nam là?
A. Huế
B. Hà Nội
C. Đà Nẵng
Đáp án: B
    Also fine: 1. or 1) numbering, a) or (A) options, a * before the correct option, or an answer key at the end (Đáp án: then 1-B, 2-A, …). In Word, type the numbers yourself: automatic numbering isn't read.
  • Options: shuffle questions or answers, bring missed questions back until you get them right, or grade only at the end.
  • Answer with a click or A–E / 1–5, then Enter for the next question. End stops early.
  • The result screen lists every wrong answer with the correct one, lets you retry them once, and keeps your score history for each set in this browser.
  • The image button at the top right sets a background picture.
